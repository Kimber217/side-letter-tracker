# Side Letter Tracker UI audit (TEST copy "blocker fixes"), 2026-11-28

Scope: test copy only (Jk9u1ikQu5Vg6Hq5ChLsmP). Driven through the screen at 1440px and 390px. Source not read, nothing published, GitHub untouched.

Data I created in the test copy: LP "TEST Marlowe County Pension" ($30M, Second closing) and letter "TEST Marlowe side letter" (pasted, 10 paragraphs, 8 terms approved). Ridgeview review finished (4 terms approved). Calder worksheet signed off, sent, 3 elections recorded. Brightwater worksheet archived then restored. Marlowe LP archived then restored. 12 draft worksheets regenerated via "Generate anyway for 9 LPs".

## 1. Verdict

For an IR head the app is promising and already faster than a spreadsheet for "what did we promise whom". Extraction from my pasted letter was largely correct (grant bases, conditions, carve-outs, MFN settings), and the audit log hash chain, integrity check and stale-worksheet warning are real strengths. But I would not rely on it for MFN decisions yet: the closing-date limit in an MFN clause is only printed as a note and is not enforced (Marlowe was "offered" terms from LPs admitted at a later closing, which its own letter excludes), numeric conditions are never evaluated ("Needs a decision" even when the arithmetic is obvious), and a fee-rate question returns a non-fee 0.00% co-invest term as a "below standard fee" answer. For a first-time founder it is usable once someone tells them the order (add LP, add letter, review, MFN), but the first screen is an empty-looking search page with no onboarding, jargon (MFN, bundle, grant basis, "Needs a decision") is unexplained, and phone width hides the Review button and most table columns.

## 2. Bugs, most severe first

### B1 (major) MFN worksheet ignores the closing-date limit in an LP's MFN clause
- Screen: MFN > Election worksheets, TEST Marlowe County Pension worksheet.
- Steps: Add LP TEST Marlowe (Second closing, size limit $30M). Paste letter whose ¶8 says the Investor may elect terms of LPs "admitted on or before the Investor's Closing Date". Approve terms, finish review, MFN > Election worksheets > Generate anyway.
- Expected: Halvorsen Family Office (Final closing, $10M) and Tamsin Holdings LLC (Final closing, $5M) were admitted after Marlowe's Second closing, so their terms are not electable by Marlowe.
- Actual: "17 electable items held by other LPs considered: 4 offered, 1 awaiting a decision, 12 not offered". The 4 offered include Halvorsen "15 Business Days' notice of capital calls", Halvorsen "Quarterly unaudited financials within 45 days (Q1-Q3)" and Tamsin "Notice of a Key Person Event within 5 Business Days". Each only carries the note "The clause also limits election to terms granted to Limited Partners admitted on or before the Investor's Closing Date, in addition to the commitment-size limit." So the restriction is shown as text, not applied. Likely cause: LP "Closing" is free text ("Second closing", "Initial Closing", "Final closing"), so nothing can be compared.
- Related: LP "Closing" is a free text box on Add LP, with inconsistent existing values ("Initial closing" vs "Initial Closing").

### B2 (major) "Needs a decision" for conditions the app could compute; contradictory badges
- Screen: MFN > Check a proposed term. LP receiving: TEST Marlowe ($30M). Term "Management fee reduced by 0.10% for commitments of $40M or more", condition "Commitment of at least $40M", granted for Commitment size.
- Expected: Pinecrest ($35M) fails a $40M floor; Brightwater ($50M), Cascade ($75M), Lindqvist ($60M), Meridian ($150M) meet it.
- Actual: all 8 LPs, including Pinecrest ($35M, own commitment), show "Needs a decision" with "Condition to check against this LP: Commitment of at least $40M. Holder committed $30M, within this LP's MFN limit of $35M (its own commitment)". Header badges read both "No LP could elect this" and "8 need a decision". The why text states the holder's $30M, which is not the number the $40M condition is tested against, so it reads as if $30M < $40M should disqualify everyone.
- Calder ($25M) correctly "Not eligible: Holder committed $30M, above this LP's MFN limit of $25M".

### B3 (major) Fee-rate search answers include a non-fee term at "0.00% (0 bp)"
- Screen: Search. Question "Which LPs pay below the standard management fee and at what rate?" (after Marlowe's letter was approved).
- Actual: "7 terms match", including "TEST Marlowe County Pension · Co-invest rights · MFN excluded · 0.00% (0 bp)" for "first look at any co-investment opportunity ... with no management fee and no carried interest". That is a co-invest vehicle term (fee rate stored as 0), not the LP's fund management fee. An IR head reading the rate column would think Marlowe pays 0% on the fund. Before Marlowe the same question returned "4 terms match" with Northgate's fee offset shown as a 5th card ("Added by keyword match: ¶ 3 mentions "management fee". Filed as Fees.") so the count (4) did not match the cards visible (5), and the offset has no rate. Meridian's rate shows only "1.25% (125 bp)" though the term is 1.25% then 1.00% on invested capital.

### B4 (major) Review: category selects cannot be changed by keyboard typing; Ridgeview ¶5 forced into "no obligation"
- Might be a harness limit, but worth a check. In Review, selecting the category of the new ¶5 term did not take (typing "Excuse" typed into the Summary field instead; the select stayed "Unclear"). Single-character key presses worked on the Add-letter LP select, so I did not conclude a bug. Because I could not set the category at the time, I resolved Ridgeview ¶5 (an excuse right tied to the county investment policy) as "no obligation" with a note. Nothing warns that an excuse right is an obligation. Ridgeview now has no excuse term in Search or MFN.

## 4. Usefulness

### What works well for IR
- Plain-English question to term cards with exact quote, paragraph number, vehicle, conditions, expiry, ongoing obligation, MFN reason and "Granted for" in one card.
- Keyword cross-check ("Every paragraph containing these words is in the results" or a list of missed paragraphs) is a strong trust feature.
- Letter vs LP record card and MFN-clause confirmation gate Finish review; Ridgeview's settings landed on its LP row ("Case by case", with the clause summary text).
- Stored original with SHA-256 matching audit log ("Integrity verified"), Audit log "Chain intact 110 entries", tamper-evident wording.
- Worksheets: "Approved terms have changed since this draft was generated. Regenerate before sign-off." Sign-off locked ("Signed off. The package is now locked."), Record as sent, per-item "LP elected / LP declined", Election records tab with who and when. "Generate anyway for 9 LPs" with a clear banner about Brannock.
- Archive/Restore works for LPs (totals went $565M to $535M and back, terms 46 to 38 and back, picker and worksheets excluded the archived LP) and for draft worksheets (with a reason, shown in "1 archived worksheet" with Restore).

### What works for a founder
- Fund settings page with standard fee (1.5%), carry/hurdle (15%/7%) and clause number; vehicles list; the app is "made up data" and says so.
- Example search chips give a way in. The Add LP form is short, and Add letter has three entry modes (upload, paste, by hand).

### Confusing for a newcomer
- Nothing says "start here". The landing page is a search box over 38 terms; there is no "set up your fund, add LPs, add letters" path and no empty-state guidance.
- Terms such as MFN basis, "commitment limit", grant basis, bundle, "Needs a decision", "Chain intact" and category keywords are unexplained.
- LP type, vehicle, closing, commitment and MFN settings must exist before the letter can be added (the LP select blocks Add letter), but the Add letter form does not link to Add LP.
- Review is dense: each draft term needs status, reason, grant basis, ongoing flag and bundle; the checkbox "None of these" for grant basis is easy to miss and blocks approval with a red line far below.
- Phone layout hides core columns.

## 5. Top 10 improvements, ranked

1. Make the closing-date limit enforceable: replace free-text "Closing" with a fund-defined list (Initial, Second, Final with dates) and apply "admitted on or before" in worksheets and checks (B1). Reason: it silently offers terms an LP is not entitled to.
2. Evaluate numeric conditions (commitment of at least $X, "equal or smaller") against each LP and return Eligible/Not eligible when computable; reserve "Needs a decision" for real judgment (B2). Fix the contradictory header badges.
3. Keep fee-rate search to fund management fee terms only; exclude co-invest, offsets and carry rows from "below standard fee" and show rate steps (1.25% then 1.00%) (B3).
4. Do not let "Mark no obligation" close an excuse or other rights paragraph without confirmation, and make the Review flag list explain what "no obligation" means (B4).
5. After approving a draft, keep scroll position and focus on the next draft; show a progress counter ("3 of 8 left") and a "approve all with no issues" step for terms with no flags (B9).
6. Add a first-run guide and empty states: Settings, Add LP, Add letter, Review, MFN, with one sentence per term of art.
7. Pre-fill vehicle and MFN status where the letter is explicit (Parallel Fund, "Excuse", co-invest) and give useful default MFN reasons instead of "The letter does not state the basis for this repo..." (B11).
8. Show readable names in the Audit log (item titles, not t:t-... ids), list what an LP archive cascaded to, and add a filter chip row (B12).
9. Make quotes cover the whole obligation and show deadlines inside the quote (Reporting ¶4, Brightwater ¶3) so IR can verify without opening the letter; add "view full letter" from a search card.
10. Phone layout: stack tables as cards, keep Review/Edit buttons visible, add tab-bar scroll cue (B13).
